# Security Policy

JurisDev Kenya takes the security of its software and communities seriously.
This policy applies to all repositories in the JurisDev Kenya organization.

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, pull requests, or discussions.

Instead, report them privately by email to:

**brikab@proton.me**

Alternatively, you may use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the affected repository (Security tab, then Report a vulnerability).

When reporting, please include as much of the following as you can:

- A description of the vulnerability and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The affected repository, version, or URL.
- Any suggested remediation, if you have one.

---

## What to Expect

- We will acknowledge your report within a reasonable timeframe.
- We will investigate and keep you informed of our progress.
- We will let you know when the issue is resolved.
- We are grateful for responsible disclosure and will credit reporters who wish to be acknowledged.

---

## Scope

This policy covers the source code and services maintained within the JurisDev Kenya organization.

Please note:

- Our projects are provided for legal education and technology purposes and do not constitute legal advice.
- Some repositories may depend on third-party services; vulnerabilities in those services should be reported to their respective maintainers.

---

## Responsible Disclosure

We ask that you:

- Give us reasonable time to investigate and address an issue before public disclosure.
- Avoid accessing or modifying data that does not belong to you.
- Act in good faith and avoid privacy violations, service disruption, or data destruction.

We will not pursue or support legal action against researchers who report vulnerabilities in good faith and in accordance with this policy.
