# Governance

## Purpose

This document describes how JurisDev Kenya is governed and how technical and community decisions are made.

Our goal is to build an open, collaborative, and sustainable legal technology community where contributors can participate transparently and confidently.

---

## Leadership

The JurisDev Kenya GitHub organization is administered by Organization Owners.

Organization Owners are responsible for:

- Managing the GitHub organization.
- Managing teams and permissions.
- Protecting community repositories.
- Supporting working group leads.

Organization ownership does not imply unilateral decision-making. Major decisions should be discussed openly with the relevant working groups.

---

## Working Groups

JurisDev Kenya currently operates through the following working groups:

- Technology
- Policy
- Education & Research
- Partnerships & Community

Each working group may appoint maintainers for repositories under its responsibility.

---

## Repository Creation

New repositories should only be created when:

- there is a clearly defined purpose;
- at least one maintainer has agreed to support the repository; and
- the relevant working group has discussed the proposal.

This helps keep the organization focused and avoids abandoned repositories.

---

## Maintainers

Repository maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing pull requests.
- Managing issues.
- Keeping documentation up to date.
- Encouraging constructive collaboration.

Maintainers are expected to act fairly and transparently.

---

## Contributors

Everyone is welcome to contribute.

Contributors are encouraged to:

- Read the contribution guidelines.
- Participate respectfully in discussions.
- Open issues before beginning significant work.
- Submit focused pull requests.

---

## Decision Making

Whenever possible, decisions should be made through open discussion.

The general approach is:

- Small repository decisions are made by repository maintainers.
- Working group decisions are discussed within the relevant working group.
- Community-wide decisions are discussed openly before implementation.

---

## Amendments

This document may evolve as JurisDev Kenya grows.

Changes should be proposed through GitHub Discussions or Pull Requests.
